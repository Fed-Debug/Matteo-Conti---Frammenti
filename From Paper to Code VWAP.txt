Here is an example of taking a research paper and turning it into a simple trading strategy to test
whether the underlying logic might produce a positive drift in the equity curve.
The code was applied to NQ data on a 1-minute timeframe.
And make sure you create and compile a custom function called exactly VWAP_RTH_Custom
using the attached code.
VWAP is one of those concepts that is heavily used on trading floors because portfolio managers
use it to evaluate the price at which execution traders are able to get their orders filled.
If they are executing a buy order for the portfolio manager, the execution trader wants to get filled at
a price lower than VWAP. If they are executing a sell order, they want to get filled at a price higher
than VWAP.
This can create a tendency for price to revert toward the VWAP of the day.
