=================================================================
N-BAR SHORT (N-Bar Low Breakout, short only)
Short Description (PowerLanguage / MultiCharts signal)
=================================================================
WHAT IT DOES
============
An intraday short-only breakout strategy. It sells short when
price breaks below the lowest low of the last N bars, stops taking
new entries after a set time, and is forced flat on the close at
the end of the day. Nothing is held overnight.
INPUTS
======
NumBars Lookback in bars for the breakout level.
Default: 5.
LastEntryTime HHMM. No new entry is armed at or after this time.
Default: 1545.
FlattenTime HHMM. Any open short is covered on this bar's close
at or after this time. Default: 1600.
ENTRY & EXIT
============
Entry: a resting sell-short stop at the lowest Low of the last
NumBars bars (using the level as it stood on the prior bar). It
fills only if price trades down through that level, and only while
the time is before LastEntryTime.
Exit: the ONLY exit is the end-of-day flatten. When the time
reaches FlattenTime, the short is covered on the close. There is
no stop loss and no profit target, so a short runs until
FlattenTime once it is open.
HOW TO INSTALL
==============
1. Open the PowerLanguage Editor in MultiCharts.
2. File > New > Signal (this is a trading signal, not an
indicator). Name it "N-Bar Short".
3. Paste in the code and press F3 (Compile).
4. Apply to an INTRADAY chart.
NOTES & LIMITATIONS
===================
• Short only. Pair it with N-Bar Long on the same chart if you
want both directions.
• Set LastEntryTime and FlattenTime in HHMM to match your
session (the 1545 / 1600 defaults suit a close around 16:00).
• No protective stop or target. Risk stays open until the EOD
flatten, which can be a full day's adverse move. Add a stop
yourself if you need one.
• Default MultiCharts order handling (one position at a time,
no pyramiding unless enabled).
