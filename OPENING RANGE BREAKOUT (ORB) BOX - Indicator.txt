=====================================================================
OPENING RANGE BREAKOUT (ORB) BOX - Indicator
MultiCharts PowerLanguage template
=====================================================================
WHAT IT DOES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
At the start of each trading session the indicator watches the first
stretch of the day (the "opening range") and records the highest high
and lowest low during that window. Once the window closes it locks that
band and draws it on the chart:
- a dashed box around the opening-range period,
- two solid horizontal lines marking the range High and Low, extended
across the rest of the session,
- an optional dotted midline,
- optional price labels.
From then on it watches for the first time price pushes beyond the High
or below the Low and marks that breakout with an arrow. It keeps only
the most recent few sessions on screen and resets at the start of every
new day.
HOW IT WORKS (STEP BY STEP)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. During the opening window (from the session open to that time plus
your chosen length) it tracks the running high and low.
2. When the clock passes the end of the window, the range is "locked"
for the day and the box and lines are drawn once.
3. The first close (or touch) above the High prints an up arrow below
that bar; the first below the Low prints a down arrow above that bar.
At most one of each per day.
4. Everything is drawn as chart objects (not plotted lines), which keeps
each day's levels separate instead of joining them into diagonals.
5. A small internal list remembers each day's objects, so when a session
scrolls past your "days shown" limit, its box, lines, and arrows are
removed automatically.
INPUTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Opening range
ORStartTime (0930) Session open, in HHMM. The range starts here.
Set to your market's open (e.g. 0930 = 9:30 AM).
ORLengthMinutes (15) How long the opening range lasts, in minutes.
15 = the 9:30-9:45 range. Use 5, 30, 60, etc.
SessionEndTime (1600) Time the High/Low lines stop extending (close).
Breakouts
UseCloseBreak (true) How a breakout counts.
true = a bar must CLOSE beyond the level
(fewer, more confirmed signals).
false = a wick/touch beyond the level is enough
(earlier, more signals).
EnableAlerts (false) Fire a MultiCharts alert the first time price
breaks each side. (Also switch alerts on in the
study's Format -> Alerts tab.)
History
MaxDaysShown (5) How many recent sessions stay drawn; older ones
are removed automatically. 0 = show every loaded
day. Maximum 20.
Display toggles
ShowBox (true) Draw the dashed box over the opening-range period.
ShowBoxSides (false) Add the box's left/right vertical edges. Off gives
a cleaner look (just top and bottom).
ShowMidline (false) Draw a dotted line at the midpoint of the range.
ShowLabels (true) Show the "OR High / OR Low" price text at the
right edge.
ShowBreakouts (true) Draw the breakout arrows.
Sizing
LineWidth (1) Thickness of the OR High/Low lines (0 = thinnest).
ArrowSize (2) Size of the breakout arrows.
ArrowOffsetFrac (0.25) How far arrows sit off the bar, as a fraction of
the range height. Bigger = more gap from the candle.
Colours
ORHighColor The range High line.
ORLowColor The range Low line.
ORMidColor The optional midline.
BoxColor The box outline.
BreakoutUpColor The up breakout arrow.
BreakoutDnColor The down breakout arrow.
USAGE NOTES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Apply to an intraday chart (1, 5, 15-minute, etc.).
- Assumes a single day session that does not cross midnight (regular
trading hours). For 24-hour / Globex data, the daily reset may need to
be tied to a session boundary instead of the calendar date.
- On a live, still-forming bar, freshly drawn objects can disappear on
the next tick unless the study is set to keep intrabar drawings.
Historical bars are unaffected.
