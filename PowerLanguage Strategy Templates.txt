This is a growing library of PowerLanguage strategy templates.
Be clear on what they are and what they are not. These are not finished strategies you can run and
expect to make money. They are building blocks. Each one is a clean, working scaffold you take,
adapt, and turn into something of your own.
The most useful way to use them is to feed them to your LLM. When you are building a strategy
and need a specific feature, a particular entry, an exit, a sizing method, drop the relevant template
into your favorite LLM as a reference. It gives the model correct, working PowerLanguage to build
on, so what comes back is closer to what you actually need and far easier to fix. Over time the point
is to build yourself a library of templates you can feed to your LLM and develop your own
strategies faster and cleaner.
What is here so far is only a small starting set. I will keep uploading new templates regularly, and
the same goes for the Indicators and Functions channels. This is the starting point, not the finished
shelf.
Take them, adapt them, make them yours.
