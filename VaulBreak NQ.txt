VaultBreak NQ - Longs Only
==========================
Long-only intraday volatility-breakout signal for NQ E-mini futures on
5-minute RTH bars. Each session it sets an upper "noise boundary" above the
open; a close above that line, while price is also above session VWAP,
triggers a long. The trade is then managed by a fixed target and stop, a
VWAP-loss exit, and a hard end-of-day flatten. Nothing is held overnight.
Instrument and Session
======================
• NQ E-mini futures, 5-minute bars
• Regular trading hours, 09:30–16:00 ET
• Long only, flat by 15:50 ET, no overnight positions
Entry (long)
============
• Fires when price closes above NoiseUp (session open plus 0.60 of the
average daily range)
• Confirmation filter: the close must also be above session VWAP
• Entry window 09:45 to 15:00, up to 3 trades per day, no pyramiding
• Needs roughly 15 sessions of warm-up before it begins trading
Exits (whichever triggers first)
================================
• Profit target: $1,000 (50 NQ points)
• Stop loss: $2,000 (100 NQ points)
• VWAP exit: two consecutive closes below VWAP
• Time exit: flat at 15:50 ET
Notes
=====
• "DailyATR" is an expanding-window average of past session ranges, not a
rolling ATR(14); after warm-up it drifts toward a long-run average rather
than tracking recent volatility
• Nominal risk to reward is 1:2, but in practice the VWAP and time exits
close most trades before either fixed level is reached
